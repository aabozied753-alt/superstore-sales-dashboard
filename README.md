# Superstore Sales Dashboard

An Excel dashboard answering one question: where does the money come from, and where does it get lost?

## Key numbers
- Total sales: $2,294,795
- Total loss (sum of losing deals): $156,131

## Key findings
- Sales are similar across the three categories, but Furniture's profit margin is about 2% versus about 17% for Technology and Office Supplies
- West and East together account for about 70% of profit
- Loss rate jumps sharply once discounts pass 20%, and at 30% almost every deal loses money
- Monthly sales are seasonal, with the highest peak in early 2016

## Dashboard sections
- Sales and profit by category
- Profit by region
- Loss percentage vs discount percentage
- Monthly sales 2014-2017
